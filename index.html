Not connected
This browser doesn't support Web Serial, so it can't talk to the Arduino directly. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer. You can still use the desk here in preview mode.

Channels

Click a channel name to rename it. Hold Flash to send the channel to full. Keys: B blackout, 1–8 recall a scene, Esc stop the fade.

Scenes

Press Record, then click a scene slot to save the current faders into it. Click a saved scene to fade to it. Shift-click a scene to delete it.
