          Categories

            Export for lights

            A timecode sheet grouped by Titan cue list — the times you type into the desk's Edit Times. To write the times onto the desk itself, use Record times to Titan below.

            Record times to Titan

            On the desk: connect the cue list, press Timecode → Record. Here: switch the clock to Internal and press play from the start. Cue Clock presses GO at each cue's time, so Titan records those exact timecodes onto the cues. Stop recording on the desk when done.

            Other exports

            Import cues

            Paste a CSV with a Timecode (or Time) column, plus optional Name, Category, Cue List, Cue, Fade, Notes.
